Getting Started

What is ethical testing?

Ethical hacking means testing a system's security with proper authorization. The goal is to find weaknesses before a malicious attacker does.

Only test systems you own or have written permission to test!

Legal background

Unauthorized testing of other networks is a criminal offense in most countries. The difference between an ethical hacker and a cybercriminal lies in legal authorization — the same port scan is legal with a contract, and a crime without one.

Safe practice: home lab (Raspberry Pi, VMs) or a CTF platform!

WiFi security levels

  • WEP – RC4-based, broken in minutes. Don't use!
  • WPA – Outdated, vulnerable
  • WPA2 – AES-based, secure with a strong password
  • WPA3 – SAE protocol, best current protection

wifite automatically tests these weaknesses.

Important ports

  • 22 – SSH (secure remote access)
  • 80 – HTTP (unencrypted web)
  • 443 – HTTPS (encrypted web)
  • 445 – SMB (Windows sharing ⚠️)
  • 3389 – RDP (Windows Remote Desktop ⚠️)

nmap shows which ports are open on your network.
