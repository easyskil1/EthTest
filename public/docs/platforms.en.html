Practice Platforms

TryHackMe (THM)

The best platform for beginners. Structured "Rooms" and learning paths guide you step by step. They have a built-in VM — no setup needed at the start.

Free basic access — sign up here first.

Hack The Box (HTB)

Industry standard. You get an IP address and need to get in — no hand-holding. "Seasonal" machines showcase the latest vulnerabilities. The Global Elite rank is recognized in the job market.

Excellent for OSCP exam preparation.

PortSwigger Academy

The bible of web security. Completely free courses and labs from the creators of Burp Suite. SQL injection, XSS, SSRF, IDOR and many more topics — with industry-level content.

VulnHub

A collection of freely downloadable vulnerable virtual machines. Ideal for offline, personal lab environments (VirtualBox, VMware).

Bug Bounty – HackerOne / Bugcrowd

If you're already confident, you can earn money. Major companies (Google, Tesla, DoD) pay for found bugs. Payouts: $100 – $100,000+. Legal, protected environment on real industry systems.

Recognized certifications

  • eJPT – eLearnSecurity Junior PT (best for beginners)
  • CEH – Certified Ethical Hacker (entry level)
  • PNPT – Practical Network Penetration Tester (TCM Security)
  • OSCP – Offensive Security Certified Professional (gold standard)
OSCP requires proving in a 24-hour exam that you can compromise machines and document the process.
